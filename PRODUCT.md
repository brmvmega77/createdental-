# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Create Dental serves clinics ordering dental laboratory work, the chief technician, employees, and the laboratory owner. The owner requires a separate authenticated cabinet; this was explicitly confirmed on 2026-10-21.

## Product Purpose
Connect orders, services and tooth selections with production, quality, communication and business oversight. The owner needs financial metrics, unit economics, workload, client health and actionable exceptions in nontechnical Russian.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing portal data includes orders, overrides, employees, clients, history and tasks. Payment fields are cumulative order snapshots, not a bank transaction ledger. Actual cost and expense recording must be explicit; missing data is never presented as zero profit or invented cash receipts. Existing clinic and staff workflows must remain intact. Synced sources are read-only.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve Create Dental's existing light, white/milk and graphite interface. No theme switch, no horizontal mobile tab scrolling, pointer cursor across entire actionable surfaces. Use existing logo assets.

## Evidence on Hand
Current repository and user-provided screenshots of clinic and staff surfaces. Production deployment and actual accounting completeness require verification, not assumption.

## Product Principles
- Show trustworthy measures and their scope.
- Connect exceptions to underlying orders.
- Keep owner access separate from clinic and staff access.
- Maintain useful mobile layouts and clear error recovery.
